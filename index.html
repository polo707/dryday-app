Getting Up at Night?

0 logged
  • Have your last big drink about 2 hours before bed, and drink enough earlier in the day.
  • Cut down on coffee, tea, cola and alcohol, especially in the afternoon and evening.
  • Empty your bladder right before you go to sleep.
  • Log night trips as Bathroom Trip → At night. If you get up 2 or more times most nights, tell your doctor: it can have causes worth checking, and your report shows these trips.

Discreet Notifications

On: reminders only say "Daily reminder · Time for your routine". Off: they say "DryDay · Your 5-minute routine is ready".

For night owls: sets done after midnight count for the day before.

How Reminders Work: DryDay sends a phone notification at each time you pick, even when the app is closed. Android: works in Chrome. iPhone: first add DryDay to your Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer), open it from its icon, then turn reminders on. Your diary and progress stay only on your phone: the reminder service only knows your times, your time zone and how many sets you did today.
